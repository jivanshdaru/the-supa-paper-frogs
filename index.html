Frog FAQ

Everything you probably wanted to know before adopting a tiny paper jumper.

How long does it take to get my frog?
Most frogs are made in small batches, so we'll let you know when yours is ready. Because every frog is folded by hand, please allow a little time for folding and testing.
Can I pick any colour paper?
Yep! Pick yellow, pink, or tell us your own colour. We'll do our best to use the paper you request.
How many frogs can I order?
As many as you like! Add them to your cart in the Frog Lab and use the + and − buttons to change how many of each one you want. When you're happy, hit Checkout and your email app opens with the whole order ready to send.
What is the difference between the frogs?
The Classic uses one sheet of A4/A5 paper. The Supa Jumper uses two sheets and has an extra-strong spring fold for a bigger jump. The Hypa Jumper uses three sheets for our highest leap — complete with a backflip.
Are the frogs really handmade?
Yes! TSPF frogs are folded by hand, with no glue or scissors, and each frog is tested before it is sold.
How do I contact TSPF?
Email us at tspf.reach@gmail.com.
